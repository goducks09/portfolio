About

Bridging the gap between engineering and the people who use it.

I found my passion for programming a few years ago while automating processes at George Fox University — and I've been hooked ever since. Since then I've been learning different languages and building projects ranging from websites to full-stack applications.

Having a background in business and customer service gives me a unique edge: I understand projects from both the technical side and the administrative and client perspective. I'm especially strong in attention to detail, problem solving, and communication — and I've always thrived both as part of a team and working independently.

Outside of work you'll find me deep into a variety of music, movies, and gaming. A self-described life-long learner.

"A degree in business, a mind for code, and an obsession with building things that actually work."
